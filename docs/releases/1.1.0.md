## Japanese support in 1.1.0

- Add offline Japanese wake phrases with an explicit recognition-language selector.
- Match kana, common kanji readings, full/half-width forms and GPT spellings. Optional kana
  readings support names and alternate pronunciations.
- Add a Japanese interface and keep controls reachable with larger text.
- Fill a short Japanese example with one tap, without a Japanese keyboard.
- Run Japanese speech recognition after VAD on a separate worker. Phrase changes, calls and Stop
  discard old results and preserve safe microphone/model release.
- Retain the existing Chinese/English default, sensitivity settings and Direct Boot preferences.
- Fetch Japanese model weights at build time with pinned checksums and package them for offline use.
- Add native speech checks to release CI and real Android instrumentation tests.

Version code: **3**. No new permissions. Japanese adds approximately 69.4 MiB of model assets
plus its reading dictionary. Say the complete Japanese phrase on its own and pause briefly;
test your chosen phrase first. Recognition accuracy and battery use vary with speech and environment.
